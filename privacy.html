Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This application is a private, personal tool used only by its owner to manage their own TikTok account. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data of any user.

Data handling

  • The App requests access only to the owner's own TikTok account, limited to the scopes required to post content and read the owner's own video statistics.
  • No data is collected from or about any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally by the owner and are not transmitted to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the owner of this application.
