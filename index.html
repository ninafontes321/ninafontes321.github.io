My TikTok Account Manager

This is a private, personal tool used solely by its owner to manage their own TikTok account. It is not a public product and is not offered to third parties.

What it does

  • Posts videos to the owner's own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Retrieves basic public statistics for the owner's own videos via the TikTok Display API.
